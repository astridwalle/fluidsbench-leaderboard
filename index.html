CFD Surrogate Models Geometric Deep Learning Open Benchmark

FluidsBeach is an open benchmark for evaluating machine learning surrogate models on computational fluid dynamics (CFD) tasks. It provides standardised datasets, evaluation protocols, and this leaderboard to enable fair, reproducible comparison of methods across diverse flow scenarios — from external aerodynamics to internal pipe flows. Datasets are sourced from CaEMLDatasets.org.
